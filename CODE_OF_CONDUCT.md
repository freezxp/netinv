# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- **Technical disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.** "This breaks when the
  agent returns zero" is useful. "Did you even test this?" is not, and the
  first version is easier to act on anyway.
- **Assume competence and missing context, in that order.** Most bad patches
  come from someone who did not know a constraint, not someone who did not
  care. The constraints in this project are frequently undocumented until
  someone trips over them.
- **Reports about hardware you own are contributions.** A person saying "your
  Huawei connector reports nonsense on my box" is doing the project a service,
  including when they are wrong about why.
- **Respect that this is maintained by one person in their own time.** Slow
  responses are not a slight.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about anyone's identity or
background, deliberate intimidation, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Also not acceptable: pasting configuration or packet captures containing other
people's network details without permission, and posting anything that would
expose a third party's infrastructure. When filing a hardware report, redact
addresses and identifiers you are not free to publish — the issue templates say
so as well.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and any
project space, including when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](../../security/advisories/new) if there is no other
private channel available, or by opening an issue asking for a private contact
without including detail.

The maintainer will act as they see fit, which may mean editing or removing
content, or blocking an account. There is no committee here and no appeals
process; there is one person trying to keep the project a reasonable place to
be. Reports will be handled discreetly, and the reporter's identity will not be
shared with the person reported without asking first.
